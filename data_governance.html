The Lifecycle In Practice

STAGE 01

Asset Discovery

Automated scans crawl every connected system to build a single inventory of tables, files and streams — nothing gets governed if it was never found.

STAGE 02

Ownership & Classification

Each dataset is assigned a business owner and a sensitivity tier, which in turn determines what controls automatically apply to it.

STAGE 03

Control Enforcement

Access rules, encryption and masking are applied based on classification — consistently, without waiting on a manual review.

STAGE 04

Continuous Assurance

Quality scorecards and compliance dashboards run on a standing basis, surfacing drift before it becomes an incident.

Roles & Responsibilities

DO

Data Owner

Accountable For The Data

Usually a business or department lead, accountable for a dataset's accuracy, appropriate use and who is allowed to see it.

  • Approves classification levels
  • Signs off on non-standard access
  • First escalation point for issues
DS

Data Steward

Responsible For The Day-To-Day

Keeps catalog entries current, runs quality checks and resolves the issues that automated monitoring flags.

  • Applies classification consistently
  • Maintains catalog and glossary entries
  • First contact for dataset questions
DC

Data Custodian

Manages The Underlying Systems

Operates the platforms data lives on, implementing the technical controls that owners and stewards define.

  • Configures access and encryption
  • Maintains backup and retention jobs
  • Executes incident response steps
